# Commercial licensing

FlatLand is dual-licensed.

## Option 1 — GNU AGPL v3 (free)

The default. See [LICENSE](LICENSE) for the full text. In short, you may use,
modify and redistribute FlatLand at no cost, for any purpose including
commercial ones, **provided that anything you build on it is itself released
under the AGPL**.

Concretely, you can use FlatLand under the AGPL if:

- You are evaluating it, learning from it, or using it for research.
- You use it **inside your own organization** and do not distribute it. Running
  FlatLand on your own parts, in your own pipeline, for a commercial product you
  sell, is free and always will be. The AGPL's obligations attach to
  distribution and to network-accessible services, not to internal use.
- You ship it as part of software that is itself AGPL-licensed, and you make the
  complete corresponding source available to your users.
- You offer it over a network **and** provide your users with the complete
  source of your modified version, as AGPL section 13 requires.

## Option 2 — Commercial license (paid)

You need a commercial license if you want to do any of the following **without**
releasing your own source code under the AGPL:

- Distribute FlatLand, or a derivative of it, inside a **proprietary or
  closed-source** product — including linking `libflatland` into a closed-source
  application, whether statically or dynamically.
- Offer FlatLand's functionality as a **hosted or network service** (a web API, a
  SaaS product, an internal service exposed to customers) without publishing the
  source of your modified version.
- Redistribute it under terms of your own choosing, or embed it in a product
  whose license is incompatible with the AGPL.

A commercial license removes the copyleft obligation. Terms are negotiated per
engagement — scope, duration, support expectations and pricing all depend on how
you intend to use it.

**To enquire:** open an issue at
<https://github.com/Interfluo/flatland/issues> titled "Commercial licensing", or
contact the maintainer through the
[Interfluo GitHub profile](https://github.com/Interfluo).

## Which one applies to me?

| What you are doing | License |
| :--- | :--- |
| Academic research, teaching, personal projects | AGPL, free |
| Internal analysis at a company, nothing distributed | AGPL, free |
| Contributing to FlatLand itself | AGPL, free |
| Shipping an open-source (AGPL) tool built on FlatLand | AGPL, free |
| Shipping a closed-source product that links FlatLand | **Commercial** |
| Running a hosted service on it without publishing your source | **Commercial** |
| Embedding it in a product under an AGPL-incompatible license | **Commercial** |

If you are unsure which column you fall into, ask before you ship. That
conversation is free and considerably cheaper than the alternative.

## Notes

- **This document is a summary, not the license.** Where it and
  [LICENSE](LICENSE) disagree, the LICENSE text governs. It is also not legal
  advice; if the distinction matters to your business, have your own counsel
  read it.
- **Earlier releases stay under their original license.** FlatLand was published
  under the GNU GPL v3 up to and including the `v2` release. That grant is
  irrevocable for those versions — this change applies from the AGPL relicensing
  commit onward.
- **Contributions.** By opening a pull request you agree that your contribution
  is licensed under the AGPL, and that the maintainer may also offer it under
  the commercial license above. Dual licensing only works if one party can grant
  both, so a contributor licence agreement may be requested for substantial
  contributions.
